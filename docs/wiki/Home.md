## What is KnightDragonX OS?

KnightDragonX OS is an **Arch-based Linux distribution** focused on the KnightDragonX brand identity. It combines:

- **Hyprland** compositor with **HyDE** tooling
- **SDDM** login manager with custom KDX Corners theme
- **KDX Hotspot** WiFi repeater service
- **Wallbash** dynamic theming
- **KDE red** (`#FF0033`) accent palette throughout
